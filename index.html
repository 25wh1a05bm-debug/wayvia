PROBLEM STATEMENT

From the Team Vertex Research Synthesis
  • • In today’s digital world, location-based platforms help people discover nearby restaurants, hospitals, and other destinations. However, finding a location does not mean knowing whether it meets an individual’s specific needs.
  • • People with mobility limitations and users with particular dietary or sensory preferences need additional information before deciding a place.
  • • This information may be scattered across maps, business websites, customer reviews, photographs, and individual listings.
  • • As a result, users may struggle to determine whether a destination offers the facilities and environment they require before travelling.
